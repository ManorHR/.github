## About us

**ManorHR (Pvt) Ltd** is a Sri Lankan HR technology company building connected products for job seekers, employers, recruitment agencies and HR teams. Our vision is to make recruitment and workforce operations more accessible, efficient and transparent through practical software.

## Shared platform capabilities

- **Candidate experience:** searchable jobs, accessible applications and mobile-first account management.
- **Employer experience:** vacancy publishing, application review and structured hiring workflows.
- **Agency experience:** multi-client recruitment operations and appropriate access boundaries.
- **Workforce operations:** reusable identity, roles, permissions, notifications and reporting.
- **Trust and privacy:** consent-aware processing, secure access, data minimization and auditability.
- **Localization:** Sri Lanka-first workflows with multilingual and international expansion paths.

## Engineering direction

Our current technical direction includes **Laravel, PHP, API-based services, progressive web applications (PWAs), modular architecture and cloud storage integrations**. Specific stacks and integrations are chosen per product and should not be assumed to be deployed across the portfolio.

We prioritize:

1. **Security and privacy by design** — least-privilege access and clear data boundaries.
2. **Practical interoperability** — documented interfaces and reusable services.
3. **Human oversight** — recruitment automation should support, not silently replace, responsible decisions.
4. **Accessible design** — fast, responsive and inclusive interfaces.
5. **Operational reliability** — observability, maintainability and testable workflows.
